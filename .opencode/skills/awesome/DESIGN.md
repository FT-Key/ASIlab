## Overview

High-contrast, expressive style with creative typography and bold color choices for visually striking interfaces.

## Style Foundations

- **Visual style:** high-contrast, artistic, bold
- **Typography scale:** 12/14/16/18/24/30/36
- **Typography fonts:** primary=Inter, display=Limelight, mono=JetBrains Mono
- **Typography weights:** 400, 500, 600, 700, 800
- **Color palette:** primary, secondary, success, warning, danger
- **Spacing scale:** 4/8/12/16/24/32
- **Border radius:** 0 (sharp corners)

## Colors

- **Primary (#3B82F6):** Acciones principales, links, highlights.
- **Secondary (#8B5CF6):** Acentos, etiquetas, elementos destacados.
- **Success (#16A34A):** Estados positivos y confirmaciones.
- **Warning (#D97706):** Estados de cautela y alertas.
- **Danger (#DC2626):** Estados de error y acciones destructivas.
- **Surface (#FFFFFF):** Fondos de pagina y cards.
- **Text (#111827):** Texto principal y contenido.
- **Ink (#111827):** Negro lacado para bordes y elementos activos.

## Artistic Characteristics

- **Borders:** Bold 2px borders in black (#111827)
- **Corners:** Sharp corners (no border-radius)
- **Shadows:** Bold offset shadows (4px 4px 0)
- **Typography:** Limelight for display, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for labels
- **Labels:** Uppercase with wide tracking
- **Hover effects:** Translate Y with shadow offset
